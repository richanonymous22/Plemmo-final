FAQ

Utility questions, answered

When should I start looking for a new energy contract?

You can usually start comparing and locking in rates up to 12 months before your current contract ends. This protects you from price changes and supports a smoother transition.

Will my power or internet be cut off during a switch?

No. For energy, the new supplier takes over the billing while the physical supply infrastructure remains the same. For broadband, we help coordinate the switch to minimize downtime where possible.

Can you guarantee I will save money?

No. We cannot guarantee savings until a formal quote comparison is completed using your actual, verified usage data. Businesses on out-of-contract rates may see stronger opportunities when switching to a fixed tariff.

Does Plemmo charge a fee for this service?

No, our comparison service is free for you to use. We may receive a commission from the supplier if you choose to switch through us.

What if I'm in an existing contract?

We can review your current contract end date and start arranging a new deal ahead of renewal, so you don't roll onto a default tariff. We can also advise on exit fees where applicable.

Can you help multi-site businesses?

Yes. If you operate multiple locations, we can review energy across all sites and look for consolidated or site-by-site options as appropriate.
