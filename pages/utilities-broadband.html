FAQ

Business energy & broadband FAQs

When should I start looking for a new energy contract?

You can usually start comparing and locking in rates up to 12 months before your current contract ends. Reviewing early protects you from price changes and makes for a smoother switch.

Will my power or internet be cut off during a switch?

No. For energy, your new supplier takes over billing while the physical supply stays exactly the same — the electricity or gas itself never stops. For broadband, we coordinate the switch to minimise downtime wherever possible.

Can you guarantee I will save money?

No. We can't guarantee savings — this can only be confirmed once a formal quote comparison is completed using your actual, verified usage data. Businesses currently on an out-of-contract rate are often the best placed to benefit from switching to a fixed tariff.

Does Plemmo charge a fee for this service?

No — our comparison service is free to use. We may receive a commission from the supplier if you choose to switch through us.

Can I switch before my contract ends?

We can review your current contract end date and start arranging a new deal ahead of renewal, so you don't roll onto a default tariff. Switching an active contract early can sometimes incur exit fees, so we'll always check your end date and advise you accordingly before anything is arranged.

How long does switching take?

It varies by supplier and meter type, but a typical business energy switch takes a few weeks from application to your new contract starting. We'll keep you updated throughout, and can usually start the process ahead of your renewal date so there's no gap in cover.

Can you help with multiple sites?

Yes. If you operate multiple locations, we can review energy across all sites and look for consolidated or site-by-site options, whichever suits your business.

Can you arrange smart meters?

Yes. We can help arrange a smart meter upgrade, or a new meter installation for new business premises, working with your supplier to get it booked in.

How much could I save?

Savings vary depending on your current tariff, usage and contract status — a specialist can confirm your exact position after a quick review. We don't display live prices or figures until we've looked at your account, so any number you see elsewhere before that point is illustrative only.
