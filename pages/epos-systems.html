FAQ

Good to know

Which EPOS system is right for my business?

The right EPOS system depends on your business type, checkout volume, stock needs and whether you need hospitality features like table or kitchen management. Tell us about your business and we'll point you to the right setup.

Can EPOS integrate with card machines?

Yes. Several of our EPOS packages include an integrated card machine as standard, and others can be paired with one depending on the provider and setup you choose.

Do you provide training?

Yes. Free setup and training are included with Plemmo EPOS packages, so your team is up and running from day one. Ongoing support is available depending on your provider and package.

Can I track stock?

Yes. Stock control — including inventory tracking and supplier management — is a core feature across our EPOS packages.

Can I use it for hospitality?

Yes. Our hospitality EPOS packages support table management, order taking, kitchen printing and reporting, with options like kitchen display screens and split bills depending on the package you choose.

Are prices final?

Indicative pricing only — your final quote depends on hardware, software, installation and support requirements, and we'll set that out clearly before you decide.
