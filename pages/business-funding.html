Funding types

14 ways to fund your business

Every business need is different. Here's where each funding product typically fits — see full details for typical amounts and terms.

Business Loan

A lump sum repaid in fixed instalments over an agreed term.

Suitable for: expansion, stock, acquisitions, general use

Secured Loan

A loan secured against a business or personal asset, typically with lower rates.

Suitable for: larger amounts, longer terms

Unsecured Loan

A loan with no asset required as security, based on business affordability.

Suitable for: businesses without assets to secure against

Startup Loan

Funding designed for newer businesses that are still establishing trading history.

Suitable for: new and early-stage businesses

Working Capital Loan

Short to medium-term funding to support everyday running costs.

Suitable for: cash flow, seasonal gaps, operating costs

Asset Finance

Funding secured against the equipment or machinery being purchased.

Suitable for: equipment, machinery, tools

Vehicle Finance

Funding to acquire commercial vehicles, vans or a small fleet.

Suitable for: vans, fleet & commercial vehicles

Commercial Mortgage

Longer-term funding secured against commercial property.

Suitable for: buying or refinancing premises

Bridging Finance

Fast, short-term funding to bridge a time-sensitive gap.

Suitable for: chain breaks, time-sensitive completions

Invoice Finance

Releases cash tied up in unpaid customer invoices.

Suitable for: businesses awaiting invoice payment

Merchant Cash Advance

Repaid automatically as a percentage of future card sales.

Suitable for: businesses with regular card payments

VAT Loan

Spreads the cost of a quarterly VAT bill over time.

Suitable for: covering a VAT bill

Tax Loan

Spreads the cost of Corporation Tax or another tax bill.

Suitable for: covering a tax liability

Revolving Credit Facility

A pre-agreed limit you can draw from, repay and reuse.

Suitable for: ongoing, flexible cash flow needs

FAQ

Business funding questions, answered

Is approval guaranteed?

No. All funding is subject to lender checks, eligibility, affordability and approval.

Is Plemmo a lender?

No. Plemmo is an introducer, not a lender — we help you explore options through selected funding partners.

What types of businesses can apply?

Most UK businesses can apply. Acceptance depends on trading history, turnover, industry and lender criteria.

How fast can funding be arranged?

It depends on the lender, the documents you provide and the approval process.

Can I apply with bad credit?

Some lenders consider a range of credit profiles, though approval is never guaranteed.

How much can I borrow?

This depends on your business profile, affordability, turnover, lender criteria and the product you choose.

Do I need security?

Some funding options are unsecured; others require security or guarantees, depending on lender terms.

Does Plemmo charge a fee?

Plemmo may receive commission from providers. Any fees charged to you would be clearly disclosed.
