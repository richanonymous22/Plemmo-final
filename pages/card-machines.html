Frequently asked questions

How long does it take to get a card machine?

It depends on your chosen provider, approval and delivery — many providers offer fast onboarding with next-working-day delivery once you're approved.

Are there any monthly fees?

It depends on the provider. Shift4 and SumUp have no monthly rental, Teya is £15/month (or a one-off terminal buyout), and Clover, Elavon and Worldpay charge a monthly device fee. We'll always confirm the exact cost before you apply.

Can I keep my existing merchant account?

It depends on your current setup and the provider you choose — our finder will never recommend the provider you're already with, so it's built around finding you something genuinely different. Speak to us to check what's possible.

What are the typical transaction fees?

Transaction fees vary by provider, card type and turnover. Our suggested rate starts from 0.35% blended — or 0.30% if you also add utility services with Plemmo — subject to business profile, turnover and provider approval.

What happens if I want to cancel?

It depends on your provider and contract. Shift4 and SumUp Pay As You Go have no fixed contract, while others — such as Teya's 12-month option or SumUp's discounted 12/24-month plans — do. We'll always confirm the terms before you apply.
